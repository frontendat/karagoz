# Handbook

The handbook explains the concepts behind Karagöz Hayali. For the exact shape of every type and function, the pages
link to the [API Reference](/hayali/api-reference), which is generated from the code comments.

* [Lesson Schema](/hayali/handbook/lesson-schema) – lessons, chapters, steps and positions.
* [Adjustments and Removals](/hayali/handbook/adjustments-and-removals) – what a step does to the files.
* [Locks](/hayali/handbook/locks) – keeping a file read-only for a step.
* [Editor Directives](/hayali/handbook/editor-directives) – opening, closing and highlighting, and `preview.url`.
* [Callouts](/hayali/handbook/callouts) – tips, warnings and notes in step bodies.
* [Navigation and Resets](/hayali/handbook/navigation-and-resets) – moving between steps without losing work by accident.
* [Layout](/hayali/handbook/layout) – side by side or one panel at a time.
* [SSR and Frameworks](/hayali/handbook/ssr-and-frameworks) – server rendering, Nuxt and Astro.
